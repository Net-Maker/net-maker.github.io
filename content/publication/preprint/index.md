abstract: "GaussiAnimate reconstructs and rigs diverse animatable categories from temporally consistent 4D Gaussian or mesh sequences. Its Skelebones representation combines the intuitive control of a kinematic skeleton with the deformation fidelity of free-form bones. A non-parametric Partwise Motion Matching method then retrieves and blends local motion to synthesize expressive reanimation."

GaussiAnimate compresses the level of dynamics of a 4D shape into compact **skelebones** that remain both controllable and expressive. See the [interactive project page](/gaussianimate/) for the method and qualitative results.
