Research direction

Making 4D representations
reconstructible and controllable.

My research lies at the intersection of computer graphics and 3D vision. I build representations that recover geometry and motion from visual observations, then organize them into controllable structures for animation. My current focus is dynamic Gaussian reconstruction, category-agnostic rigging, and non-rigid motion synthesis.
