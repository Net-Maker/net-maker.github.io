PhD Student Westlake University

I am a PhD student at Westlake University, advised by Prof. Yuliang Xiu. I studied Computer Science at Hangzhou Dianzi University (2020–2024), where I began my research journey with Prof. Weichen Dai, before joining Westlake as a Research Assistant (2024–2026) under the joint mentorship of Yuliang Xiu, Anpei Chen, Cheng Lin, and Zhiyang Dou.

My work focuses on representations and reconstruction for 3D and 4D worlds. I am always open to new collaborations—and equally happy to exchange ideas about cooking.
